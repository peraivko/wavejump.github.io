04 / BEYOND THE BUILD

the beginning.

Wave Jump is still in development. The current build is the foundation—not the finish line. There is room to push the idea further, sharpen the experience, and take these two worlds somewhere new.

BUILDING WHAT'S NEXT
NOW / THE FOUNDATION

Two worlds, one challenge

The current build features seven chapters and 59 playable levels, with world swapping, wall jumps, momentum, force blocks, and a built-in level editor.

ON THE HORIZON / PLANNED

More ways to bend the rules

Potential directions include new mechanics, more varied challenges, and fresh ways to combine the worlds. The exact scope is still taking shape.

THE LONG GAME / TO EXPLORE

A bigger universe to jump through

More visual polish, new surprises, and a more complete journey are possibilities as development continues. This is a work in progress—not a promise of specific features or dates.
